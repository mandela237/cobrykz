# Cobrykz Mobile Solutions Hub Prototype Review

## Review gate

The Solutions hub implements the approved Chaptered Atlas direction at 390px
without changing the frozen content, information architecture, section order,
calls to action, SEO, or desktop composition.

## Mobile composition decisions

- Eight numbered chapters turn the information-dense hub into a sequence of
  focused decisions.
- Business outcomes, capabilities, starting points, operating context, process,
  and trust principles keep their lead ideas visible while supporting details
  use progressive disclosure.
- The six capabilities remain visible as an indexed ledger rather than six long
  cards.
- The Capability Relationship Atlas becomes the defining dark stage. It
  preserves the shared title, description, four layer meanings, branching
  relationships, legend, reading direction, and equivalent nonvisual
  explanation.
- The shared mobile header and compact footer keep the Solutions hub cohesive
  with the approved Homepage prototype.

## One system with page differentiation

The Homepage leads with a dark business-transformation opening. The Solutions
hub begins as a light capability-exploration experience and develops a denser,
decision-led rhythm. Both use the same typography, palette, chapter markers,
architectural rails, disclosure language, header, footer, and System Atlas
materiality.

## Validation

- Full test suite: 120/120
- TypeScript: pass
- ESLint: pass
- Production build: pass, 20 pages
- Browser validation: pass at 320, 375, 390, 430, 767, and desktop 1440px
- Keyboard, focus, reduced-motion, touch sizing, unique IDs, and containment:
  pass
- Dedicated code/spec review: pass
- Dedicated visual review: pass with one non-blocking polish note

## Non-blocking polish note

The opening static relationship strip may initially resemble tabs because its
three cells use equal borders and colored underlines. Its meaning can be made
more explicitly diagrammatic during the eventual site-wide craftsmanship pass.
